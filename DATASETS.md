# Finding data

Your idea comes first. Then find public data that fits it, ideally two or more files that connect (for example, artworks and artists), under about 50 MB, downloadable without a login. Ask the AI to help you search, or start here:

| Source | What is there |
| --- | --- |
| [Seattle Open Data](https://data.seattle.gov) | City data: permits, pet licenses, 911 calls, parks, and more (Export > CSV) |
| [King County Open Data](https://data.kingcounty.gov) | County data: restaurant inspections, property, health, and more |
| [TidyTuesday](https://github.com/rfordatascience/tidytuesday) | A new dataset every week since 2018, many with several files |
| [FiveThirtyEight](https://github.com/fivethirtyeight/data) | Hundreds of datasets behind news stories |
| [data.gov](https://catalog.data.gov) | US federal data |
| [Chinook music store](https://github.com/lerocha/chinook-database) | A classic multi-table sample: artists, albums, tracks, invoices |
