# Step 1. A visible website for your idea (15 minutes)

Write your idea in [IDEA.md](../IDEA.md) first. Then ask the AI:

> Read IDEA.md and AGENTS.md. Write public_html/index.html: a simple, good-looking page for my app with a title, one sentence on what it does, and the input or drop-down the user will use. Fill the results area with 3 made-up example rows for now; we will connect real data later. Then give me the exact commands to create ~/public_html/myapp on vergil and copy the page there.

Run the commands it gives you (you will log in to vergil with your NetID).

**Checkpoint:** open `https://students.washington.edu/<your NetID>/myapp/` on your phone. Your idea is on the web.
